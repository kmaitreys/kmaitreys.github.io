Hi! I am **S. Maitrey**, a Graduate Student entering my PhD in Astronomy and Astrophysics at the [Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics (KIAA), Peking University](https://kiaa.pku.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on **Planet Formation**, **Protoplanetary Disk Modeling**, and **Astrochemistry**. I build models to study planet-forming regions and trace the chemical and physical evolution of gas and dust from molecular clouds to planetary systems, using observational constraints from facilities like [ALMA](https://www.almaobservatory.org/) and [JWST](https://webbtelescope.org/).

Previously, I worked as a project fellow at the [School of Earth and Planetary Sciences, NISER Bhubaneswar](https://www.niser.ac.in/).

As I move to China, I have also chosen a Chinese name: 沙宇仁, read as Shā Yǔrén. 沙 (Shā) derives from my family name but also refers to sand or *fine grains*, in other words, the building blocks of rocky plantesimals. 宇 (Yǔ) means the universe and 仁 (rén) denotes humaneness or kindness, alluding to the Sanskrit meaning of my given name.
