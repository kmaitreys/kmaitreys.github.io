## About Me

I have worked as a project fellow at School of Earth and Planetary Sciences, NISER Bhubaneswar, on planet formation. My research focuses on **Planet Formation**, **Protoplanetary Disk Modeling**, and **Astrochemistry**. I build models and use models to study planet forming regions and I am particularly interested in tracing the chemical and physical evolution of gas and dust from molecular clouds to planetary systems.

Currently I am studying planet-disk interactions and disk substructures to better understand the observational signatures produced by still-forming planets that we can observe from facilities like ALMA and JWST.

I will be starting my PhD at the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics at Peking University in Fall 2026.
