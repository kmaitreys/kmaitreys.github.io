Hi! I am S. Maitrey, a Graduate Student entering my PhD in Astronomy and Astrophysics at the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics (KIAA), Peking University.

My research focuses on Planet Formation, Protoplanetary Disk Modeling, and Astrochemistry. I build models to study planet-forming regions and trace the chemical and physical evolution of gas and dust from molecular clouds to planetary systems, using observational constraints from facilities like ALMA and JWST.

Previously, I worked as a project fellow at the School of Earth and Planetary Sciences, NISER Bhubaneswar.
